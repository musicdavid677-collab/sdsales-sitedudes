SiteDudes CRM — Requirements Analysis and UI Design

A complete plan for upgrading the existing Laravel CRM into a remote sales management system, based on the client's written requirements and supplied screenshots.

The new CRM (sdsales.sitedudes.com) currently provides Profile, Dashboard, Leads, Tasks, Users and Roles. The old sales system (sales.sitedudes.com) shows sales tiers, pricing, commissions, projects, leads and supporting tools. The new solution must consolidate the required features.

After a verified Moneris payment, create or link the customer in the SiteDudes intranet, send the welcome email to the customer and notifications to service@sitedudes.com and websites@sitedudes.com, then record the sale with a To Be Paid commission.

8. Remaining client requirements

Old CRM page and data migration

Audit the old CRM's Sales and Admin pages, including Dashboard, Pricing, Commission, Projects, Tasks, Support, Leads and Knowledge Base. Identify which pages, records, workflows and permissions must be migrated into Laravel. Do not assume that screenshots capture every feature.

ZoomInfo lead imports

Support ZoomInfo-sourced leads through a CSV import first or a direct integration if access and API permissions are available. Preserve source attribution, external IDs, ownership and duplicate handling.

Promo codes

Provide an administrator interface to create, activate and expire promotional codes, and a sales representative interface to apply authorized codes. Support discount type, amount, eligibility, redemption limits and usage history. Confirm whether discounts affect the commissionable setup-fee base.

Phase 2 / 3: AI email content

AI-generated sales email content is explicitly deferred. Keep it outside the initial delivery scope, while designing the messaging system so the feature can be added later.

10. Questions to confirm with the client

  1. What exactly distinguishes a Prospect from a Lead, and when does conversion occur?
  2. Which specific old CRM pages, records and permissions must be carried over?
  3. What is the precise CSV format and how should duplicate ZoomInfo records be handled?
  4. Does a sale count toward the tier at payment approval, customer conversion or another event?
  5. Does the new commission tier apply immediately to the qualifying sale or only subsequent sales?
  6. What is the exact SiteDudes Intranet API/interface for customer creation?
  7. Which Moneris integration method and merchant environment are available?
  8. Are commissions based on setup fees before or after discounts, taxes and refunds?
  9. What is the cutoff date for payouts on the 21st, and how are refunds/chargebacks handled?
  10. What are the promo code rules, privacy requirements for photo IDs and approved terms document?
Scope recommendation: First build registration/approval, prospect and lead management, and old CRM feature migration. Then implement Moneris conversion and commission payout workflows. Leave AI email generation for a later phase as requested.
